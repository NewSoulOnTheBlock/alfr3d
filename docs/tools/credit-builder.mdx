The **credit-builder** skill lets ALFR3D work on **your own credit**: audit your
FICO profile, plan score improvements, generate and mail dispute letters as USPS
certified mail (via [Lob](https://lob.com)), track 30-day response deadlines, and
plan business credit. It is a native port of the ElizaOS credit-builder plugin.

<Warning>
**Your own credit only.** This is designed for the operator's own credit — not a
service sold to others (which would make you a Credit Repair Organization under
CROA, with contract/disclosure obligations). It is **not legal or financial
advice**. Only dispute items you have a good-faith basis to believe are
inaccurate; frivolous or knowingly false disputes are unlawful under the FCRA.
</Warning>

## How it works

Unlike the MCP integrations, this ships as an ALFR3D **skill** with bundled Python
scripts (`skills/credit-builder/scripts/`) that the agent runs. Its only external
dependency is **Lob** for certified mail; the FICO analysis runs on the profile
you provide (it does not pull from the credit bureaus).

## Setup

Set `LOB_API_KEY` (from [dashboard.lob.com](https://dashboard.lob.com)) in the
environment — e.g. under `environment:` in `docker-compose.yml`, or in
`config.json`:

```yaml
environment:
  LOB_API_KEY: 'test_xxx'   # a test_ key mails nothing; switch to a live key when ready
```

Profile and dispute history persist in `~/alfr3d/credit/` (on the mounted volume).

## Using it

Just talk to ALFR3D — it selects the skill and runs the scripts:

- **"Analyze my credit"** → runs the FICO-factor audit (payment 35%, utilization
  30%, age 15%, mix 10%, inquiries 10%) and returns strengths, weaknesses,
  ranked disputable items, and a prioritized action plan.
- **"Dispute the ABC collection with a debt validation letter"** → generates the
  letter and mails it certified (Return Receipt) via Lob, then tracks the 30-day
  deadline.
- **"Check my disputes"** → shows pending vs. overdue disputes (an item the
  bureau fails to verify in 30 days must be deleted under FCRA § 611).
- **"Help me build business credit"** → coaches the DUNS → net-30 vendor
  tradelines → PAYDEX → bank/SBA ladder.

## Letter types

19 types spanning FCRA, FDCPA, FCBA, ECOA, HIPAA, and negotiation — e.g.
`basic_bureau`, `609_verification`, `611_reinvestigation`,
`method_of_verification`, `identity_theft`, `debt_validation`, `cease_desist`,
`pay_for_delete`, `goodwill`, `chargeoff_removal`, `unauthorized_inquiry`,
`hipaa_medical`, `statute_of_limitations`, `intent_to_sue`, `billing_error`.

## Cost & autonomy

Each real certified letter costs ~$9 (~$27 to all three bureaus). Sending is
**autonomous** — ALFR3D does not pause for confirmation before mailing — so keep
`LOB_API_KEY` on a `test_` key while rehearsing, and only fund/enable live
mailing once you trust the flow. Review dispute activity regularly.
